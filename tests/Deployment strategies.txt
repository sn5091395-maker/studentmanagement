Deployment strategies:- defines how a new version of an application is released to the users

1)blue-green deployment(current to upgrade)
2)canary release
3)rolling 


1)Blue-green deployment:- 
	**Two separate environments:-Blue(current version) and Green (new version).only one receives production traffic at a time.
	
	process:-
			1)Blue is running(v1)	
			2)create green environment
			3)deploy v2 to green
			4)test green
			5)switch traffic to green
			6)green becomes production
			7)keep blue for rollback
Advantages:-    1)fast rollback
		2)New version tested before the switch
		3)Minimal production downtime
		4)Easy to understand	

		
2)Canary Release:-Release the new version to a small percentage of users first,monitor it,then expand gradually.
	1)DEPLOY V2 -> 2)SEND 5% TRAFFIC -> 3)MONITOR
	

ADVANTAGES:- 1)Few users exposed at first
		2)problems detected early
		3)Lower risk a full release
		4)Easy to stop rollout

3)ROLLING UPDATE:-Gradually replaces instance running the old version -one by one ot in small 
            	groups
ADVANTAGES:-----1)no second environment needed
		2)usually the lowest infrastructure cost
		3)gradual,controlled replacement
		4)built,into Kubernetes deployments
